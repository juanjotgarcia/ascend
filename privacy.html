Zelum Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Zelum is a training app for calisthenics and the gym, with habits, nutrition targets and progress tracking. This policy explains what data the Zelum app and website handle, why, and the choices you have. Questions: zelumcorp@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Zelum works fully without an account. Then everything stays on your phone or computer.
  • If you sign in with Google, a copy of your Zelum data is backed up so you can sync devices.
  • We show no ads, use no third-party tracking, and never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and backup in the app at any time: Me → Account → Delete account & cloud backup.

1. Data that stays on your device

Your profile (name or nickname, height, body weight, goals), workouts, sets, personal records, habits, nutrition entries, notes and settings are saved in the app's storage on your device. If you don't sign in, this data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases it, so use Export backup (Me → Settings → Your data) if you want to keep a copy.

2. If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It exists only to back up your progress and sync it between your devices. When you sign in:

  • Google sign-in (Firebase Authentication, provided by Google) gives Zelum your name, email address, profile photo and an account ID.
  • Cloud backup (Cloud Firestore, provided by Google) stores a copy of the Zelum data described in section 1, linked to that account ID.
  • Only you can read or change your backup: access rules allow each signed-in person to reach their own data only. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored on Google Cloud servers.

We use this data only to run these features: signing you in, restoring your progress and keeping your devices in sync.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Zelum offers optional paid plans. In the Android app:

  • Google Play handles the payment. Zelum never sees your card or bank details.
  • RevenueCat, our subscription service provider, receives the purchase receipt from Google Play and an app user ID (your Zelum account ID if you are signed in, otherwise a random ID) to confirm which plan you have. It does not receive your name, email or training data.

Google and RevenueCat keep purchase records as needed for billing, refunds, tax and accounting. You manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

4. Music colors (optional)

Zelum can tint its colors to match the album you are playing. This is off until you turn it on.

  • Android, "Any app": with the notification access you grant, Zelum reads the title, artist and cover of the song playing in your music app. This happens on your phone only. Zelum reads no other notifications and never sends song information anywhere.
  • Website, Last.fm: if you enter a Last.fm username and API key, your browser asks Last.fm directly for your current track. Last.fm's own privacy policy applies to that request.

5. Notifications and reminders

Workout timers, rest alerts and habit reminders are scheduled on your device. They are not sent through our servers. During a workout, Android shows a live notification with your timer; it stops when you finish.

6. Website hosting

Like any website, the servers that deliver Zelum's web pages (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) process your IP address and basic request details to send the pages to you. We do not use these logs to identify or track you.

7. What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your personal data with anyone for their own purposes.

If this ever changes (for example, to add privacy-friendly usage statistics), we will update this page first and say so in the app.

8. Deleting your account and data

In the app: Me → Account → Delete account & cloud backup. This immediately and permanently deletes your cloud backup and your Zelum sign-in. Data on your device stays until you reset it (Me → Settings → Your data → Reset) or uninstall the app.

Without the app: email zelumcorp@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Zelum account". We delete your sign-in and cloud backup within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted: your account ID, name, email, photo and every piece of backed-up Zelum data. What is kept: purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat, as required by law. Nothing else is kept.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Access and copy: Export backup gives you all your Zelum data as a file.
  • Correct: edit your profile and entries in the app.
  • Delete: see section 8.
  • Stop syncing: sign out at any time. The app keeps working offline.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law. Email us and we will help.

10. Children

Zelum is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top. Important changes are also announced in the app.
